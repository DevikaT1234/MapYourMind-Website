Stress Management

Stress can affect your thoughts, emotions, relationships and everyday life. Counselling can help you explore what is causing stress and understand how it is affecting you.

Sessions can provide space to identify difficult situations, recognise patterns and consider healthier ways of coping with overwhelming feelings and pressures.

Ask About Counselling

Anxiety

Anxiety may involve persistent worry, fear, nervousness or feeling overwhelmed. Counselling provides a supportive, non-judgemental environment in which you can talk about these experiences.

You can explore thoughts and feelings that may be contributing to anxiety and develop a greater understanding of your experiences.

Ask About Counselling

Relationships

Relationships can sometimes become difficult because of communication problems, changing circumstances, emotional difficulties or disagreements.

Counselling can provide a confidential space to explore communication, boundaries, emotions and relationship patterns, helping you understand what you would like to change or improve.

Ask About Counselling

Self-awareness

Self-awareness involves developing a clearer understanding of your thoughts, feelings, behaviours and patterns of interaction.

Counselling gives you time and space to reflect on your experiences, understand yourself more clearly and consider personal goals.

Ask About Counselling

Addiction

Addiction can affect emotional wellbeing, relationships, work and everyday life. It can be difficult to talk about these experiences, particularly when there are feelings of shame or judgement.

Counselling provides a confidential and non-judgemental space to explore experiences associated with addiction and consider what support may be appropriate.

Ask About Counselling

Bereavement

Bereavement and grief can affect people in many different ways. There is no single way to experience the loss of someone important.

Counselling can provide a supportive space to talk about loss, explore emotions and work through your experiences at your own pace.

Ask About Counselling

Couples Therapy

Couples therapy can provide partners with a structured and supportive environment in which to explore relationship concerns.

Sessions may provide space to discuss communication, conflict, emotional difficulties, trust and other concerns affecting the relationship.

Ask About Counselling

Work-related Stress

Workplace pressures can affect emotional wellbeing, relationships, sleep, concentration and everyday life.

Counselling can provide a confidential space to explore workplace stress, understand how it is affecting you and reflect on ways of managing difficult experiences.

Ask About Counselling

Neurodiversity

Neurodiversity describes the natural variation in how people's brains work and how they experience the world.

Counselling can provide a supportive space to explore personal experiences, emotional wellbeing, relationships and challenges associated with neurodiversity.

Ask About Counselling

Trauma

Difficult or traumatic experiences can have a lasting impact on thoughts, feelings, relationships and everyday life.

Counselling can provide a confidential and supportive environment where experiences can be explored at a pace that feels appropriate for you.

Emergency Support

MapYourMind counselling is not an emergency or crisis service.

If you or somebody else is in immediate danger, contact the emergency services by calling 999.

If you require urgent mental-health support, please contact an appropriate urgent mental-health or crisis service in your area.
